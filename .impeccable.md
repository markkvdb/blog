## Design Context

### Users
This site is primarily for curious readers landing on Mark van der Broek's personal corner of the internet. They are here to get a sense of who he is, what he cares about, and then move naturally into reading blog posts and exploring projects.

### Brand Personality
Personal, thoughtful, playful. The site should feel like a welcoming home for technical writing rather than a polished corporate portfolio.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-mode first with a mostly white base, restrained ocean-blue accents, and a neo-brutalist edge. The interface should feel crisp and memorable, with personality in the buttons and surfaces, but without loud rainbow colors or heavy gradients.

### Design Principles
1. Make the homepage feel like a personal home first, not a generic content template.
2. Use bold, flat shapes, clear borders, and confident shadows instead of decorative gradients.
3. Keep the color palette restrained: white, ink, and ocean-blue accents with strong contrast.
4. Let typography carry warmth and personality while keeping the reading experience calm.
5. Guide people toward blog posts and projects without overwhelming them with options.
